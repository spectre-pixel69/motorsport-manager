# Economy System — Budget & Cash Flow Reference

The economy system models team finances: budget allocation, purse distribution, salaries, and cash flow tracking. Every decision has a cost.

---

## Seasonal Budget Overview

**Total Capital**: $2,500,000 (fixed per team, per season)

### Budget Allocation Buckets

| Category | Typical % | Notes |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| Rider Salaries | 40-50% | Base salaries + appearance fees |
| Staff Costs | 15-20% | Coaching, mechanics, engineers |
| Bike Hardware | 15-20% | Engine/chassis leases, upgrades |
| Equipment | 5-10% | Tires, consumables, repairs |
| R&D Investment | 5-10% | Training facility, tech unlocks |
| Reserved Buffer | 5-10% | Emergency fund, mid-season adjustments |

### Example: $2.5M Budget Breakdown

```
Rider Salaries          $1,000,000  (40%)
Staff Costs              $500,000   (20%)
Bike Hardware            $500,000   (20%)
Equipment Maintenance    $200,000   (8%)
R&D Investment           $200,000   (8%)
Reserved Buffer          $100,000   (4%)
────────────────────────────────────
Total                  $2,500,000
```

## Rider Salary Structure (Annual, Per Class)

### Base Salary Floor

| Class | Minimum | Notes |
|-------|---------|-------|
| 350 Pro (4S) | $400,000 | Premier class, highest earners |
| 250 Men (2S) | $200,000 | Mid-tier |
| 250P Restricted | $100,000 | Development class |
| Women's 250 | $100,000 | Equal to 250P |

**Appearance Fee**: $1,000 per rider per round (20 rounds = $20k/season)

**Pro Debut Bonus**: $25,000 (one-time, first-time pros only)

**Bench Rider Retainer**: $50,000/year (reserve roster, not racing)

### Performance Bonuses

| Bonus | Amount (350 Pro) | Notes |
|-------|------------------|-------|
| Win Bonus | $75,000 | Per victory |
| Podium Bonus | $20,000 | Per top-3 finish |
| Championship Bonus | $250,000 | End-of-year, class champion only |

**Other Classes** (proportional):
- 250 Men: 53% of 350 amounts
- 250P/Women's: 27% of 350 amounts

### Contract Types

**Multi-Year Contracts**:
- Length: 1-4 years
- Salary paid annually (no mid-contract inflation)
- Signing bonus: Lump sum (upfront)
- Release clause: Buyout value (0 = none, can't sell mid-contract)

**Example Contract** (350 Pro Top Rider):
```
Base Salary:           $400,000 (annual)
Signing Bonus:         $100,000 (paid year 1)
Win Bonus:             $75,000 per win
Podium Bonus:          $20,000 per top-3
Championship Bonus:    $250,000 (if wins class)
Length:                3 years
Release Clause:        $500,000 (can buy out for $500k)
Status:                No. 1 Rider (team lead)
Teammate Veto:         Yes (can block signings)
────────────────────────────────────
Year 1 Base:           $600,000 (salary + signing bonus)
Year 2-3 Base:         $400,000 (salary only)
Total Potential:       $1.5M + performance (if wins championships)
```

## Budget Management Strategies

### Aggressive (High-Risk) Budget Allocation

```
Rider Salaries:      50%  ($1,250,000)  ← Super-star signing
Staff Costs:         20%  ($500,000)    ← Elite engineers
Bike Hardware:       15%  ($375,000)    ← Latest upgrades
Equipment:           8%   ($200,000)
R&D Investment:      5%   ($125,000)    ← Minimal facility investment
Reserved:            2%   ($50,000)     ← Very risky!
```

**Outcome**: High-performer riders, new tech, but **no buffer for emergencies**. Bad season = financial collapse.

### Conservative (Low-Risk) Budget Allocation

```
Rider Salaries:      35%  ($875,000)    ← Mid-tier riders
Staff Costs:         15%  ($375,000)
Bike Hardware:       20%  ($500,000)    ← Solid components
Equipment:           8%   ($200,000)
R&D Investment:      12%  ($300,000)    ← Invest in long-term
Reserved:            10%  ($250,000)    ← Emergency fund
```

**Outcome**: Steady team, good R&D path, resilient to setbacks. Slower title contention but sustainable.

### Balanced (Recommended)

```
Rider Salaries:      42%  ($1,050,000)
Staff Costs:         18%  ($450,000)
Bike Hardware:       18%  ($450,000)
Equipment:           8%   ($200,000)
R&D Investment:      8%   ($200,000)
Reserved:            6%   ($150,000)
```

## Special Events & Adjustments

### Championship Bonus Payout (End of Season)

Top finisher in each class gets championship bonus:
- 350 Pro: $250,000
- 250 Men: $150,000
- 250P/Women's: $50,000

**Impact**: Winning team takes ~$1.1M home instead of break-even year.

### Injury Recovery Costs

If rider injured mid-season (not included in base budget):
- Medical treatment: $10,000 - $50,000 (per injury)
- Substitute rider rental: $50,000 - $100,000 (per round, mid-season)

Teams with higher fitness-trained riders have fewer injuries.

### Part Failures (Unbudgeted)

A major engine failure requires replacement:
- New engine: $75,000 (emergency cost, outside budget)
- Emergency pit prep: $10,000

Teams with higher reliability parts avoid this cost.

## Budget Management Tips

1. **Front-load expenses**: Lock in good staff, riders early (cheaper then).
2. **Monitor cash flow**: If you drop below $50k remaining, you're one bad race from problems.
3. **Invest in facility**: Level 3 facility ($200k unlock) pays for itself in 2-3 seasons of better training.
4. **Negotiate long contracts**: 3-year deals lock in salaries; better than year-to-year re-signs.
5. **Diversify income**: Chase sponsorships, finish-position bonuses, win bonuses (all additive).
6. **Plan for reliability**: Buy reliable parts to avoid emergency failures.

## FAQ

**Q: Can a team go bankrupt?**  
A: Not in current implementation. Budget is fixed per season. Future: yes, with financial consequences.

**Q: Can I fire a rider mid-season?**  
A: Not yet. Riders have multi-year contracts (can't break them).

**Q: What if I run out of budget mid-season?**  
A: Budget is allocated; you can't spend beyond it. You're restricted to planned expenses.

**Q: Do sponsorships change per season?**  
A: Yes. Higher prestige teams attract better sponsors next year. Lower prestige teams lose sponsors.

**Q: Can I take a loan?**  
A: No. Budget is fixed. This is a future feature.

**Q: How do I earn more money?**  
A: Finish higher in standings, win races, win championships. Better results = more sponsorships and purse bonuses.

**Q: What's the most expensive decision I can make?**  
A: Signing a $400k+ rider to a long contract with large signing bonus. That's ~25-30% of annual budget in year 1.
